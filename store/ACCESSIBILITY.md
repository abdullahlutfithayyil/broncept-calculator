# Accessibility Statement — Broncept Calculator

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Broncept is committed to making Calculator usable by everyone, including people who are blind or have low vision,
are deaf or hard of hearing, have motor or dexterity differences, or have cognitive or learning disabilities.

**Standard we aim for:** WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

## What the app does
- **Screen readers (TalkBack):** every key has a clear name; the pending operator is announced as "pressed";
  typed numbers, operators, results and errors are spoken ("12 times 3 equals 36", "Error. Can't divide by 0").
- **Voice control (Voice Access):** keys can be activated by saying what you see: "tap 7", "tap plus", "tap AC".
- **Low vision:** high-contrast text and key outlines in both themes, a very large result display, follows light/dark system
  setting, follows your system font size (up to 150%), zooming is not blocked, and a strong focus outline.
- **Deaf / hard of hearing:** the app has no audio, video or sound-only alerts — every message is shown as text.
- **Motor:** large touch targets (about 72 px in portrait, never below 48 px), no gestures, no timing
  requirements, no drag or multi-finger actions. Works with Switch Access and an external keyboard.
- **Keyboard:** Tab reaches every key; Enter/Space activate the focused key; digits, + − * / . % = and Escape
  work as shortcuts. There are no single-letter shortcuts, so speech dictation cannot trigger actions by accident.
- **Reduce motion:** animations are turned off when the system "remove animations" setting is on.
- **Cognitive:** one screen, no ads, no pop-ups, consistent layout, plain-language error message.

## Known limitations
- Automated testing cannot cover everything; if you hit a barrier, please tell us (contact below).
- Font scaling is capped at 150% to keep keys readable inside their circles.
